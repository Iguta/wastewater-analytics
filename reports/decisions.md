| D1 | 2026-11-07 | CUSUM is capped at `S_max = 12` (2 × default `h_i`), for pumps and for the station-wide signal. `STATION_WIDE_CHANGE` opens at `S_station ≥ h_w` and closes when `S_station < 1.0`, the same hysteresis as pump `WATCH`. | Without a ceiling, a real loss drove `S` past 100, so the incident stayed open for months after the pump was back (44371 P1, June–October 2022). With the cap, recovery takes at most about 2–3 weeks of normal days. | `params.toml`; PR #7 |
| D2 | 2026-11-07 | Sibling term subtracts only a drop the other pumps share: `min(0, median change of the OTHER sufficient pumps)` (`sibling_mode = "down_only"`). The literal §4.5 rule is kept as `sibling_mode = "spec"`. | On a 2-pump station, subtracting the other pump's deviation when it is *up* makes a healthy pump look like it dropped. Example: 44371 P2 raised INVESTIGATE in April 2022 while its capacity was +2%. | 2022 dev only, below |
| D3 | 2026-11-07 | Inhibition is computed on the capacity change `log ci − own baseline median`, and then scaled by the pump's own MAD: `u = (dev_p − min(0, median dev_others)) / scale_p`. It is not computed as `z_p − z_others`. | Pumps have different noise, so the same 20% common drop gives different z per pump (−9.0 vs −7.6). `z_p − z_other` then leaves a false per-pump drop: a synthetic setpoint change escalated to INVESTIGATE 24 days later. In capacity space, a shared drop cancels exactly. | `test_sibling_inhibition_blocks_station_wide_shift`. 2022 dev G6 (INVESTIGATE incidents per pump per 180 d, limit 1.0; 44371 / 45385 / 45078): spec rule 0.74 / 0.99 / 0.66; D2 + D3 0.49 / 0.99 / 0.66.
